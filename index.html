Three things email can't do

A letter that carries value, not just words.

Live

Money in the letter

Every bMail carries its stamp as a real payment to the person you write to. Anyone can also pay name@bmailx.com directly, wallet to wallet, with no bank in between.

Live

Proof

Each letter is sealed with the sender's own key and opens only with yours. You know who it came from, and if a single word were changed on the way, it wouldn't open.

Coming

Agreements

Send an agreement, you both sign, and it's sealed with a lasting record of who signed and when. Not live yet: see the status list below.

PayPal started as a way to send money to an email address: the money travelled beside the message. In bMailX, the letter itself carries the money.

Spam pays you

Set a price to reach you.

Strangers pay your price to land in your inbox, and the stamp is yours to keep. Friends always get in free.

  • Penny post to start. Everyone begins at 1¢. Raise it whenever you like.
  • Friends free, on top. People you know skip the stamp and sit at the top.
  • Under your price goes to Requests. Look when you feel like it, or never.
  • Reply paid. Cover their reply so they can answer you for free.
